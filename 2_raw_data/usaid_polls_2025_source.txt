SOURCE OF usaid_polls_2025.csv

Transcribed 2026_1110 from Roper iPoll study records. Every 2025 survey in the
iPoll archive that asked about shutting down USAID. The April 2026 KFF poll used
for the main figure is not included here.

Searched the Longstanding Methods and Recently Developed Methods collections on
the keywords "USAID" and "Agency for International Development". The Washington
Post item does not use either phrase and was found by reading that study's
question list, so a keyword search alone misses it.

	Roper #31122394	Ipsos Poll on Executive Orders
					Feb 7-10, 2025; n = 1,013 national adults; web
					"To what extent do you support or oppose the following
					policies?...Efforts to dismantle the United States Agency
					for International Development (USAID)."
					Strongly support 22, somewhat support 15, somewhat oppose
					24, strongly oppose 34, skipped 5

	Roper #31122387	CNN Poll, conducted by SSRS
					Feb 13-17, 2025; n = 1,206 national adults; phone and web
					"Please indicate whether you think Donald Trump doing each
					of the following is a good thing, a bad thing, or
					neither...Attempting to shut down entire United States
					government agencies, such as the US Agency for
					International Development (USAID) and the Consumer
					Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB)"
					Good thing 28, bad thing 53, neither 19, no opinion *
					NOTE: bundles USAID with the CFPB

	Roper #31122388	Washington Post-Ipsos Poll
					Feb 13-18, 2025; n = 2,601, this item asked of half the
					sample (about 1,301); web
					"Do you support or oppose each of the following?...Shutting
					down the United States agency that provides humanitarian
					aid to low-income countries"
					Support 38, oppose 59, no opinion/skipped 3
					NOTE: describes USAID rather than naming it

	Roper #31122407	KFF Poll: February 2025 Health Tracking Poll on Health
					Information and Trust, conducted by SSRS
					Feb 18-25, 2025; n = 1,322 national adults; phone and web
					"How much, if anything, have you heard about the Trump
					administration's plans to significantly downsize USAID?"
					A lot 37, some 25, a little 19, nothing at all 19
					NOTE: awareness only. This study asks about the expected
					consequences of the cuts (questions 10-14) but never asks
					whether people approve of them, so it has no support or
					oppose figure to report.

	Roper #31122408	Reuters/Ipsos Pre-Joint Session Survey
					Mar 3-4, 2025; n = 1,174 national adults; web
					"To what extent do you support or oppose the following
					policies?...Closing down the United States Agency for
					International Development (USAID), the federal body that
					administers civilian foreign aid"
					Strongly support 22, somewhat support 19, somewhat oppose
					21, strongly oppose 34, skipped 4

	Roper #31122581	Pew Research Center: American Trends Panel Wave 166,
					conducted by SSRS
					Mar 24-30, 2025; n = 3,605 national adults; phone and web
					"Do you approve of each of the following? The United
					States...ending most United States Agency for International
					Development (USAID) programs"
					Strongly approve 22, somewhat approve 13, somewhat
					disapprove 15, strongly disapprove 30, not sure 19

COLUMN NOTES
	support		Support, approve, or "good thing", combining strong and somewhat
	oppose		Oppose, disapprove, or "bad thing", combining strong and somewhat
	neither		"Neither", "not sure", "no opinion" or skipped, whichever the
				poll offered. These are not the same quantity across polls:
				CNN's 19 is a substantive "neither good nor bad", Pew's 19 is
				"not sure", and the Ipsos and Reuters figures are item
				nonresponse.
	asked_of	Respondents asked this item, which is half the sample for the
				Washington Post poll and the full sample elsewhere
	comparable	1 if the poll asked a support or oppose question
