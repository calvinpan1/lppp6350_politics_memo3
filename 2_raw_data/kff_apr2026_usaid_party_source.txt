SOURCE OF kff_apr2026_usaid_party.csv

Transcribed 2026_1110 from the Roper iPoll study record for
KFF Poll: April 2026 Health Tracking Poll [Roper #31125187],
https://ropercenter.cornell.edu/ipoll/study/31125187/questions

For each of questions 42, 44 and 45 the figures come from the
"Demographic Crosstabs" tab, "Party ID" panel, with "Include unweighted
Ns in table" checked. Percentages are weighted (WEIGHT_PID_ADJ);
Ns are unweighted.

Question wording (common stem):
	"As you may know, the (Donald) Trump administration has made several
	changes to the U.S. approach to foreign aid and global health, including
	decreasing spending and eliminating the U.S. Agency for International
	Development (USAID). Do you think these changes have had a positive
	impact, a negative impact, or no impact on..."

	Q42 (Q22_A) ...the U.S. budget deficit?
	Q43 (Q22_B) ...the ability to keep infectious disease from spreading to
	            the U.S.?  [not used]
	Q44 (Q22_C) ...the health of people in developing countries?
	Q45 (Q22_D) ...how people around the world view the U.S.?

Party ID is Q48: "In politics, as of today, do you consider yourself a
Republican, a Democrat, or an independent?" Leaners (Q49) are not folded in.

	Mode:			RDD cell and landline telephone (CATI) and web
